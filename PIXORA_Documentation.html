PIXORA is a high-speed, 100% client-side, air-gapped AI photo culling and album curation studio designed for professional wedding photographers, studios, and album designers. It eliminates hours of manual photo selection by instantly detecting eye blinks, camera blur, dark/blown exposures, and duplicate burst shots while preserving emotional heritage and newborn baby portraits.

1. Core Capabilities & Intelligence

100% Client-Side Privacy (Air-Gapped)
Zero bytes uploaded to any external server or cloud. All neural inference runs directly inside the client's browser using WebAssembly & GPU acceleration. Full privacy for high-profile weddings.
Multi-Face Interactive Picker
When a reference photo contains multiple subjects, PIXORA detects every face (up to 64 people) and renders clickable square reticles with thumbnail cards to lock the target subject.
Dual-Metric Biometrics (Zero False Matches)
Employs L2 Euclidean Distance (≤ 0.20) and relative facial proportions across 12 biometric landmarks. Completely eliminates accidental stranger matches from web images.
Baby & Infant Eye Culling Immunity
Recognizes baby and infant cranial morphology. Sleeping babies are celebrated as prized portraits and are never falsely flagged as closed-eye blinks or flaws.

3. Professional Workflow Features

  • VIP Person Finder ("Find Me"): Allows instant filtering and dedicated ZIP export of all good captures of any guest, bride, groom, or VIP in an album.
  • AI 50 Best Album Highlights: Automatically samples balanced, hero-tier captures across the full wedding timeline for instant album layout design.
  • Burst Twin Side-by-Side Comparison: Side-by-side inspection with synchronized zoom to verify facial focus between duplicate shots.
  • In-Browser Multi-threaded ZIP Export: Streams full-resolution curated albums directly into compressed archives without leaving the workstation.
  • Seamless Keyboard Navigation: Fast culling via keyboard shortcuts (G for Good, B for Bad, Del for Trash, Arrows for Lightbox navigation).
