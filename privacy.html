Privacy Architecture

Effective Date: November 2026 • Published by AB Designing

1. The Zero-Knowledge Guarantee

At AB Designing, we hold user privacy as a sacred principle. When you import 10 or 10,000 photos into PIXORA, your files remain strictly inside your device's random access memory (RAM). No photo binary is ever sent to AB Designing or stored on any central server.

2. Local Computer Vision Sandbox

Laplacian focus scoring, perceptual hashing (dHash), and facial landmark blink evaluations are executed via local client-side WebAssembly. You can disconnect your device from the internet entirely and PIXORA will continue to process your photos without interruption.

3. Proprietary PIXORA Neural Engine (100% In-Memory)

All biometric landmark mapping, facial proportions, VIP person matching, and aesthetic pose evaluations run directly on your GPU/CPU via WebAssembly. PIXORA relies on zero third-party cloud AI vendors or remote inference APIs. Your personal portraits and wedding albums remain strictly air-gapped on your device.

4. No Tracking or Fingerprinting

PIXORA contains zero third-party advertising trackers, marketing pixels, or user behavior telemetry.

Questions regarding our privacy architecture? Contact AB Designing via our digital studio team.
